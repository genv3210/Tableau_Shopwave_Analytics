## 🔍 Report Sections

### 1. Sales Performance Overview
- Revenue grew consistently from 2020 to 2023 with clear seasonal patterns
- Peak sales in **March, June, September, and December** driven by promotions and festive periods
- **Consumer segment** leads at 51.50% of total revenue; **Central region** tops geographically at $2.8M
- Best-selling products: Chairs (Furniture), Storage (Office Supplies), Phones (Technology)

### 2. Profit Analysis
- Total profit doubled from **$248.9K (2020)** to **$504.4K (2023)**
- **Copiers** are the most profitable product; **Tables** consistently incur losses despite strong sales
- **China** leads country-level profitability at $150,535
- Consumer segment contributes **51.06%** of total profit

### 3. Logistics Efficiency
- **40.59%** of shipments are delayed — exceeding the 38.63% on-time rate
- Standard Class shipping has the highest late-delivery rate despite the lowest cost-to-sales ratio (8.11%)
- Shipping costs surged from **$34K (Q4 2020)** to **$159K (Q4 2023)**
- **Chad** has the highest per-order shipping cost at $149.90 due to low order volume

### 4. Customer Acquisition & Retention
- Steady growth in customer acquisition across all regions; **Canada** shows the slowest growth
- **Q1 2021** saw a -29.90% decline in customer growth, attributed to COVID-19 disruptions
- Consumer segment has the highest **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**
- Opportunities exist to strengthen Corporate and Home Office segments via targeted programmes

### 5. Business Outlook & Forecast
- Sales and profit projected to continue growing through 2027
- Widening forecast error bars signal potential **economic volatility**
- **Canada's** profit margin declined from 30.37% (2021) to 25.88% (2023)
- Copiers and Phones remain top profit generators; Tables and Bookcases continue to underperform

## 💡 Key Recommendations

| Area | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Pricing** | Implement dynamic pricing models; adopt tiered discounting for high-value customers |
| **Product Strategy** | Conduct strategic review of Tables; prioritise high-margin items like Copiers and Phones |
| **Logistics** | Shift non-urgent orders to Standard Class; establish regional fulfilment centres |
| **Shipping Costs** | Renegotiate bulk carrier contracts; optimise routes with AI-driven planning |
| **Customer Retention** | Launch loyalty programmes targeting top CLV customers in each segment |
| **Market Expansion** | Deploy region-specific campaigns for Canada and the Home Office segment |
